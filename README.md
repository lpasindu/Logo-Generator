## Logo Generator (Made-In Badge Generator)

A specialized tool designed for e-commerce websites and online sellers who import products and resell them locally. This application automatically overlays clean, professional country-of-origin badges and custom manufacturer stamps onto product photography to maintain transparency and boost buyer trust.

---

### Key Features

* **Batch Processing:** Quickly apply standardized origin stamps (e.g., "Made in Japan," "Imported from USA") across multiple product images simultaneously.
* **Customizable Badges:** Tailor badge shapes, font styles, color palettes, and opacity to seamlessly match your brand identity without obscuring key product details.
* **Flexible Positioning:** Easily place badges in any corner or designated border area of your product frames.
* **E-Commerce Ready:** Outputs high-resolution images optimized for fast web loading and maximum clarity across desktop and mobile storefronts.

### Use Case

Ideal for localized retail and import businesses operating in a specific country that need a streamlined, automated way to display compliance-friendly manufacturing labels directly on their marketing and catalog assets.
